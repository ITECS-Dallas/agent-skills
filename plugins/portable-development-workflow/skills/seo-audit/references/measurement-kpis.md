# SEO Measurement & KPIs Reference

## Overview

Pure rankings and traffic volume are incomplete measures of SEO success. Effective measurement connects search visibility to business impact, content quality, technical health, brand demand, and, where relevant, AI-search visibility.

## The New Measurement Framework

### Tier 1: Business Impact (What Ultimately Matters)
- **Revenue from organic**: Track conversions and revenue attributed to organic search
- **Qualified leads from organic**: Lead volume and quality from organic traffic
- **Customer acquisition cost (CAC) from organic**: Compare to paid channels
- **Organic conversion rate**: Are you attracting the right traffic?

### Tier 2: Search Visibility (How You're Found)
- **Organic traffic**: Total and segmented by intent type (informational, commercial, transactional)
- **Keyword rankings**: Position tracking for target keywords, especially commercial/transactional
- **SERP feature presence**: Featured snippets, People Also Ask, local packs, shopping features, AI-generated answers, or other features relevant to the market
- **Click-through rate (CTR)**: From Search Console — are impressions converting to clicks?
- **Impressions**: Total search visibility even without clicks

### Tier 3: AI & Brand Visibility (The New Frontier)
- **AI citations**: Is your content referenced in AI-generated answers?
- **Share of Model (SoM)**: What percentage of AI responses mention your brand vs. competitors?
- **Branded search volume**: Are more people searching for your brand name?
- **Brand mention growth**: Tracked across web, social, and AI platforms
- **AI referral traffic**: Sessions from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI platforms

### Tier 4: Engagement & Quality (Are Visitors Valuable?)
- **Session quality metrics**: Pages per session, time on site, scroll depth
- **Bounce rate by page type**: High bounce on service pages = problem; on blog = may be normal
- **Return visitor rate**: Are people coming back?
- **Goal completions**: Form fills, calls, downloads, sign-ups per session

### Tier 5: Technical Health (Is the Foundation Solid?)
- **Core Web Vitals scores**: LCP, INP, CLS across field and lab data
- **Index coverage**: Indexed pages vs. submitted pages
- **Crawl errors**: 404s, server errors, crawl anomalies
- **Page speed scores**: PageSpeed Insights, Lighthouse

## Essential Tools Setup

### Free / Required
- **Google Search Console**: Rankings, impressions, CTR, index coverage, CWV, structured data errors
- **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)**: Traffic, behavior, conversions, user journeys
- **Bing Webmaster Tools**: Bing-specific search data, indexing, diagnostics
- **Google Business Profile Insights**: Local discovery, actions, engagement

### Recommended Paid Tools
- **Ahrefs or Semrush**: Rank tracking, backlink monitoring, competitor analysis, site audit
- **Screaming Frog**: Technical audit and ongoing monitoring
- **PageSpeed Insights / GTmetrix**: Performance monitoring
- **Call tracking**: CallRail, CallTrackingMetrics, etc. for phone lead attribution
- **AI visibility tools** (emerging): AthenaHQ, Peec AI, Visalytica for GEO tracking

## Reporting Cadence

### Weekly Monitoring
- Keyword ranking changes (top 20 target keywords)
- Index coverage errors in Search Console
- Core Web Vitals field data
- Mobile vs. desktop SERP feature changes
- Review monitoring (new reviews, ratings changes)

### Monthly Reporting
- Organic traffic trends (total, by page, by landing page type)
- Conversion and lead attribution from organic
- Backlink profile changes (new referring domains, lost links)
- Content performance (top pages, growing pages, declining pages)
- Local SEO metrics (GBP impressions, actions, ranking in local pack)
- Competitor movement analysis

### Quarterly Deep Analysis
- Full technical SEO audit
- Content audit (identify pages needing refresh, consolidation, or removal)
- Backlink audit (identify toxic links, new opportunities)
- AI visibility assessment (manual querying of AI platforms)
- Strategy review and priority adjustment
- Branded search volume trends

### Annual Review
- Comprehensive competitor analysis
- Content strategy overhaul
- Full site architecture review
- SEO ROI analysis vs. other channels
- Goal and KPI framework revision

## Setting Realistic Expectations

### Timeline for Results
- **Technical fixes**: Can show results within weeks (crawling/indexing improvements)
- **On-page optimization**: 2-4 weeks for re-indexing, 1-3 months for ranking impact
- **Content creation**: 3-6 months for new content to mature and rank
- **Link building**: 3-6 months for authority signals to accumulate
- **Local SEO**: 1-3 months for GBP optimization, 3-6 months for local pack ranking
- **Significant organic growth**: 6-12 months for most businesses
- **Compounding returns**: SEO compounds over time — each action builds on previous work

### Benchmarking
- Compare against your own historical performance first (month-over-month, year-over-year)
- Competitor benchmarking for share of voice and ranking positions
- Industry benchmarks for CTR by position, conversion rates, and traffic patterns
- Account for seasonality and algorithm updates when evaluating performance

## Attribution Considerations

### Multi-Touch Attribution
- Organic search often plays a role early in the customer journey (research phase)
- Last-click attribution undervalues SEO's contribution to revenue
- Use GA4's data-driven attribution model to understand SEO's true impact
- Track assisted conversions to see where organic search contributes without getting last-click credit

### AI Search Attribution
- AI-generated answers often don't result in clicks but do influence brand awareness
- Track branded search lifts as a proxy for AI visibility impact
- Monitor "dark traffic" (direct visits that may originate from AI recommendations)
- Set up UTM parameters where possible for AI platform referrals

## Stop Tracking These (Outdated Metrics)

- **Keyword density**: Irrelevant since 2015; focus on topical coverage instead
- **PageRank**: Google stopped publishing this publicly years ago
- **Domain Authority as an absolute**: DA/DR are third-party metrics, not Google metrics — use for trends, not absolute targets
- **Raw traffic volume alone**: Without conversion context, traffic is vanity
- **Rankings for a single keyword**: Search is too personalized and variable; track keyword groups and overall visibility
- **Bounce rate in isolation**: It's contextual — a quick, accurate answer can have a high bounce rate and still be excellent content

## Reporting Best Practices

- **Lead with business impact**: Revenue, leads, conversions first; then ranking/traffic details
- **Contextualize changes**: "Organic leads rose because a high-intent service page gained visibility for several commercial queries"
- **Compare against goals**: Set quarterly targets and report against them
- **Note external factors**: Algorithm updates, competitor changes, seasonality
- **Include next steps**: Every report should end with prioritized action items
- **Segment data**: Break down by service line, location, content type — aggregate numbers hide insights
